How We Work

A clear path from first contact to a running system and ongoing support

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We discuss your goals, current tools, and processes. You get a clear picture of what Bitrix24 can do and what we recommend for your case.

  2. 2

    Planning

    We agree on scope, timelines, and deliverables. You see the plan before we start so there are no surprises.

  3. 3

    Implementation

    We set up and customize Bitrix24: data migration, pipelines, automation, and integrations according to the plan.

  4. 4

    Training

    We train your team so they can use the system confidently from day one. Materials and follow-up Q&A are included.

  5. 5

    Support

    After go-live we stay available for questions, fixes, and improvements. You can extend support as your needs grow.
